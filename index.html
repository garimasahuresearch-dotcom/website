(01) — About

I study how plants heal — isolating, standardising and formulating natural products into therapies for diabetes, chronic wounds, inflammation and the nervous system.

Garima Sahu is an Assistant Professor at the United Institute of Pharmacy, Prayagraj, where she teaches pharmacognosy and leads research at the meeting point of phytochemistry and pharmaceutical technology.

Before Prayagraj she spent five years at the Bharat Institute of Technology, Meerut. Her recent work loads plant extracts into PLGA nanofiber scaffolds for diabetic wound healing, and maps how dietary polyphenols, flavonoids and propolis behave in the body.
